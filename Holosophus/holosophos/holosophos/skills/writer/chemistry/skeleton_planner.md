# Plan the chemistry article

Before drafting save paper_skeleton.md: working title, question, article type, central claim, data and literature sources, method, evidence per claim, section order, missing information, limitations and supplementary materials. Match structure and relative section emphasis to the available evidence. State planned work as planned. Keep core evidence in the main text.

Record the requested total PDF page budget, including references unless explicitly excluded by the user or requested venue. Record whether the target is approximate or a strict user/venue limit. Plan around approximate targets without turning them into hard limits. Place secondary analyses in supporting files.
