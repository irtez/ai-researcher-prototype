# Check the complete article

Check question-to-answer consistency, contribution-to-evidence alignment, methods sufficient for reproduction, honest limitations, units and terminology across prose/tables/figures, citation integrity and supported conclusions. Check every required section from the skeleton, resolve TODOs or report blockers. Compile and inspect the current PDF. Run all three writer-tools analyzers for the same revision and fix substantive ordering/placement findings. A failed or incomplete pass cannot finalize clean; the tool imposes no minimum page count. Check total PDF length, including references unless explicitly excluded. Allow small deviations from approximate targets for necessary content and readability; do not repeat layout-only revisions just to meet an approximate count. Report unresolved deviations from explicit strict limits. Preserve readable body and figure fonts rather than shrinking them to fit. Preserve and update the registered article files and their bibliography in place (defaults: paper.tex, references.bib and paper.pdf).
